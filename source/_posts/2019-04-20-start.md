The starting point of this blog.

<!--more-->

## Why blogging?

- Information translation
- Personal knowledge management
- Idea sharing

There are so much fragmented information scattering all around, for example good recipes, coding techniques, news, etc.. Each time I find something useful, I save them in my hard disk, as if I process them. I collected a lot information, and tried hard to organized them with many categories, using different tools, in various media, as pretty as I can. I gradually realized that they are saved only in my computer, not my brain.

I know there is a missing link, the most important one. I never share them. The best way of interpreting something is to share them. I need a way of output, to translate information to knowledge.

{% asset_img DIKW_pyramid.png DIKW (abbreviation of Data, Information, Knowledge, Wisdom) pyramid from David McCandless %}

<!-- [DIKW](DIKW_pyramid.png) -->

It is not only a personal blog, but also a knowledge management system for myself.

Unlike my previous containers (Evernote, Onenote, Endnote, pdfs...) for information, a blog will be able to share my ideas to others, no matter how silly those ideas are.
